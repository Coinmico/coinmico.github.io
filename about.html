About Coinmico

Market data as a public utility.

Coinmico is a global crypto market data platform. We measure the market from the source, from exchange trade streams and on-chain pools, and publish it openly for everyone.

Our mission

Give everyone the same view of the market that the venues have.

So that a trader in Jakarta, Lagos or São Paulo reads the same honest number as a desk in New York. We index trades instead of accepting reported figures, label anything we did not measure, and leave a field empty rather than fill it with a guess.

Our vision

The reference index the crypto market settles on.

Market data that is independent of any exchange, verifiable by anyone, and available on every screen: web, phone and API.
