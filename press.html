Short boilerplate

Coinmico is an independent crypto market data platform that measures prices, volume and liquidity from the exchanges and blockchains it indexes itself.

Long boilerplate

Coinmico is a global crypto market data and analytics platform. Instead of republishing figures that exchanges report about themselves, Coinmico primarily computes prices, volume, rankings and candles from trades it collects directly from exchange streams and on-chain pools, excludes outliers and mispriced markets, and documents every rule in a public methodology. Coinmico is available on the web in six languages and on Android.
